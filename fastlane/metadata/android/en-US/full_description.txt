Use a tablet from your phone, or any Android device from another, over Wi-Fi or a VPN. The controlled device only needs ADB over the network (adb tcpip 5555) or a USB OTG cable. No app is installed on it.

- Works on Android 16
- The picture fills the phone screen. The tablet can switch to the phone's resolution while connected
- The tablet's resolution and display size are always restored after disconnecting, even when the connection drops
- One floating control panel: nav bar, auto-rotate, mouse, screen off, screenshot, power, device status
- Audio forwarding (Android 12 or later on the controlled device)
- Settings are remembered per device

Based on EasyControl (易控) by mingzhixian and yutils, which builds on scrcpy.

Port 25166 has no authentication. Use it on a trusted network or over a VPN.
